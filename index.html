    Which type should I choose?
    • Adverse event: something happened that harmed, or could have harmed, a patient, visitor or staff member.
    • Near miss: an error was caught before it reached the patient.
    • Nonconformity: a procedure, policy or requirement was not followed or not met (e.g. QC not done, form missing).
    • Complaint: a patient, relative or staff member is unhappy with the service.
    • Improvement opportunity: no event, but something could be done better or more safely.

    Schedule

    Tap the days on the calendar (or switch to Select a range), choose the modality and hours, then add. You can select days across several months.

    Leave both times blank for a whole-day duty. An end time earlier than the start time is an overnight shift.
    Admin · Modality operating hours

    For modalities that follow another one (e.g. CT follows X-ray): enter the days and hours the modality runs, from the tech roster. Whoever is on duty for the lead modality at that time is shown as covering it.

    Leave both blank for 24 hours. For split shifts, add the days twice with each set of hours.
    Admin · Rotation (e.g. 7-day blocks, 24/7)

    Creates whole-day (24 h) duties in blocks: the 1st radiologist takes the first block, the 2nd the next, and so on, repeating. Use it when you move to a full rotation; "replace" clears the old schedule from the start date.
